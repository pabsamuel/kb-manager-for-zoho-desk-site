Keep your knowledge base easier to maintain.

Check article links, preview bulk text replacements, find older articles to review, and export maintenance results from Zoho Desk.

Pre-release: This documentation describes the prepared extension. It has not yet been submitted to or approved by Zoho Marketplace. There is no public install link.

Review, apply, and recover

Create a replacement preview before writing to Desk. Review each result after applying. Undo uses an organization-specific browser recovery record and checks for later edits before restoring article content.

Link checks with clear limits

Inspect internal references and choose whether to check external links. Confirmed failures and restricted or inconclusive checks are shown separately. Link scanning does not bypass login or site restrictions.

Give older articles a review

Choose an age threshold based on article modification dates and export a CSV maintenance queue. Age is a review signal, not a judgment of content accuracy.
